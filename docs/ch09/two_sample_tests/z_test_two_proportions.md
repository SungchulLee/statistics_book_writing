## Overview

Tests whether two population proportions differ.

## Test Statistic

$$
Z = \frac{\hat{p}_1 - \hat{p}_2}{\sqrt{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})(1/n_1 + 1/n_2)}}
$$

where $\hat{p} = (X_1 + X_2)/(n_1 + n_2)$ is the pooled proportion under $H_0: p_1 = p_2$.
